## Source and reuse method

Photographs were selected from Wikimedia Commons using the official MediaWiki imageinfo API. The file-specific author, source-page URL and license metadata are preserved in `content/article-images.json`. The gallery renderer must show the supplied credit and license wherever an image appears. Public-domain metadata is retained as a source claim, not an independent legal warranty.

Source guidance: [Wikimedia Commons reuse instructions](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia) and [MediaWiki imageinfo API](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Imageinfo). CC BY images require attribution; CC BY-SA images additionally retain their stated share-alike license for adaptations. Each file's source page remains the authority for its specific terms.

The files are the JPEG thumbnails returned by Wikimedia for a requested width of 1200 pixels (typically the service's 1280-pixel tier). No generative editing, added text, watermark removal or content cropping was performed. Existing photographer marks and capture dates remain. CSS may fit the photograph within a display frame. The source image's stated license continues to apply to the image; it does not relicense the surrounding article.

Validation: 100 distinct source-page URLs, 100 distinct original-file URLs, 100 distinct source SHA-1 values and 100 distinct downloaded SHA-256 hashes. Total download size: 28,847,781 bytes. All files decode as JPEG. A perceptual difference-hash check found no image pair at Hamming distance 5 or below. All 100 thumbnails were reviewed in four contact sheets; replacement candidates were also opened individually.

Excluded during review: illustrated packing graphics, paintings, document scans, identified AI-generated material, irrelevant search matches, duplicate files and near-identical candidates. A close-up bed image was replaced by a complete hotel-room view. Individual photos remain illustrative and may have been captured before the article's publication date.
